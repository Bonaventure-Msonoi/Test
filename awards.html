Presidential Awards

Honouring excellence at the IZIC Gala

The 2nd Edition Presidential Gala celebrates outstanding innovation and contribution to Zambia’s investment landscape.

Get nominated

The Presidential Gala recognises investors, enterprises, and partners advancing Zambia’s growth agenda through bankable projects, job creation, export expansion, and responsible business practice.

Nominations and applications are coordinated by the IZIC Secretariat at the Zambia Development Agency ahead of the December 2026 conference.
