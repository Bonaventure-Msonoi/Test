Invest in Zambia

Priority sectors

The Zambian Government welcomes investors across sectors, with incentives designed to raise investment and international trade.

Mining

Zambia is among the world’s major copper producers and holds significant known copper reserves, alongside cobalt and critical minerals. Opportunities include exploration, mining, and local value addition / beneficiation.

Tourism

Growth in hotels and experiences across Livingstone, Lusaka, and wildlife destinations — including Victoria Falls, national parks, and game management areas covering millions of hectares.

Manufacturing

Nearly 8% of GDP and rising, with priorities in food processing, textiles, engineering, wood products, metals, pharmaceuticals, chemicals, plastics, and building materials.

Energy

Hydro remains the backbone of generation, with large unexploited potential plus solar, wind, biomass, and storage opportunities to meet industrial and rural demand.

Agriculture

About 42 million hectares of arable land with only a fraction cultivated each year, abundant water for irrigation, and strong potential in farm blocks and agro-processing.
