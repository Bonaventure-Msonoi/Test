Getting from Kenneth Kaunda International Airport to the Kenneth Kaunda International Conference Centre.

Kenneth Kaunda International Conference Centre

The Kenneth Kaunda International Conference Centre (KKICC) is an ultra-modern facility in Lusaka. Named after Zambia’s first president and commissioned in 2022, it supports major international summits.

  • Main auditorium: 2,500+ delegates
  • Large banquet hall
  • 15 specialised conference rooms
  • Modern delegate amenities

Kenneth Kaunda International Airport (KKIA)

Zambia’s main international gateway, with expanded Terminal 2 capacity for domestic, regional, and international flights. From KKIA, delegates typically transfer by hotel shuttle, taxi, or arranged conference transport into Lusaka toward KKICC.
