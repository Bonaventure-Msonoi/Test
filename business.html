Invest in Zambia

Starting a business in 7 steps

Foreign investors may incorporate a sole-director company and register a fully owned local company without Zambian shareholders under the Companies Act, 2017.

Step 1

Check company name uniqueness

Clear the name with PACRA (Patents and Companies Registration Agency). Name search and reservation fees apply.

Step 2

Declaration of compliance

Companies Form 11 must be signed before a Commissioner of Oaths, then register with PACRA using the required incorporation forms and fees. Foreign branches use Form 46 with home-country incorporation documents.

Step 3

Obtain a tax ID

Register with the Zambia Revenue Authority using your Certificate of Incorporation.

Step 4

Register for social security

Register with the National Pension Scheme Authority (NAPSA) using the Certificate of Incorporation.

Step 5

Register for VAT (if required)

Required when taxable supplies exceed ZMW 800,000 in twelve months or ZMW 200,000 in three consecutive months.

Step 6

Investment certificate (optional)

ZDA issues an investment registration certificate after premises inspection. Processing and certificate fees apply.

Step 7

Bank account & sector licences

Open a bank account after foreign taxpayer registration, then obtain any sector-specific permits (including excisable goods licences where relevant).
