Travelers Info

Travel & visas

Practical guidance for international delegates attending IZIC in Lusaka.

Entry

Visa requirements

Visa requirements depend on nationality. Many travellers may obtain a visa on arrival or e-visa; others require advance approval. Confirm current rules with the nearest Zambian mission or official immigration channels before travel.

Support

Invitation letters

Registered delegates who need an invitation / confirmation letter for visa processing can request support from the IZIC Secretariat after completing registration.

Arrival

Airport & transfer

Arrive via Kenneth Kaunda International Airport (KKIA). Transfer options include hotel shuttles, taxis, and arranged transport to the Kenneth Kaunda International Conference Centre and partner hotels.
